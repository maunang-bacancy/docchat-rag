Sample Chai Co. — Cafe Handbook (Fictional Demo Document)

Note: This is a fictional sample document created only to demonstrate this app.
"Sample Chai Co." is a made-up placeholder name. All names and details are invented
and do not refer to any real business.

About Us:
Sample Chai Co. is a fictional cafe chain founded in 2019 in Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
It runs 15 imaginary outlets across Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Karnataka. Tea leaves are
sourced from Assam and Darjeeling, and spices are ground fresh at the Ahmedabad kitchen.

Hours:
All outlets are open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM. On Sundays they open at
9:00 AM and close at 10:00 PM. The Ahmedabad flagship stays open until midnight on Fridays
and Saturdays.

Loyalty Program:
Members earn 2 points for every 10 rupees spent. Collecting 200 points earns one free
cutting chai or masala chai. Points expire 12 months after they are earned. Membership is
free through the Sample Chai Co. mobile app.

Menu Highlights:
The signature drink is the Kesar Elaichi Masala Chai, made with saffron and cardamom.
Dairy-free options (oat and soy milk) are available for an extra 20 rupees. Popular snacks
include vada pav, samosa, and khakhra. The current seasonal special is the Winter
Adrak-Gud Chai, made with ginger and jaggery.

Returns and Refunds:
If a customer is unhappy with any drink, it will be remade or refunded, no questions asked,
within 20 minutes of purchase. Packaged tea boxes can be returned unopened within 7 days
with a valid bill.

Careers:
Sample Chai Co. hires tea baristas and kitchen staff year-round. New joinees complete a
10-day paid training program. Starting pay is 18,000 rupees per month, plus a share of tips.
Employees get one free meal per shift and a 30 percent discount on all packaged products.

Festivals:
During Diwali and Uttarayan (kite festival), all outlets offer a special festive combo of
masala chai with fafda-jalebi. Outlets are closed only on Holi and Diwali main day.
